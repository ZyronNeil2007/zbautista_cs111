My Songs

Zyron Neil A. Bautista

Introduction

Music has a special way of providing comfort, healing, and soundtracking the most meaningful seasons of our lives. Whether through poetic acoustic melodies, dreamy indie pop, or emotional vocals, here are five of my favorite songs that hold deep personal meaning.

1. Mystery of Love - Sufjan Stevens

Song Details

  • Title: Mystery of Love
  • Artist: Sufjan Stevens
  • Soundtrack: Call Me by Your Name (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack) (2017)
  • Genre: Indie Folk / Acoustic

Why I Love It & Song Meaning

Sufjan Stevens' delicate fingerpicking and ethereal, whispered vocals capture the delicate beauty, wonder, and vulnerability of falling in love for the very first time. It explores how love can feel both sacred and fleeting—like an unraveling ancient mystery that changes you forever.

2. BLUE - Billie Eilish

Song Details

  • Title: BLUE
  • Artist: Billie Eilish
  • Album: HIT ME HARD AND SOFT (2024)
  • Genre: Alternative Pop / Art Pop

Why I Love It & Song Meaning

A masterpiece of atmospheric sound design and raw emotional vulnerability. The song moves from melancholic reflection into an orchestral, haunting second half, dealing with emotional coldness, lingering nostalgia, and trying to let go of someone who couldn't be saved.

Listen to Song

3. we can't be friends (wait for your love) - Ariana Grande

Song Details

  • Title: we can't be friends (wait for your love)
  • Artist: Ariana Grande
  • Album: eternal sunshine (2024)
  • Genre: Synth-pop / Dance-pop

Why I Love It & Song Meaning

Infused with pulsating synth-pop grooves reminiscent of Robyn, this track captures the bittersweet realization that two people must part ways even when love remains. It explores the heartbreak of waiting for love in another lifetime while preserving self-respect in the present.

4. Sailor Song - Gigi Perez

Song Details

  • Title: Sailor Song
  • Artist: Gigi Perez
  • Release Year: 2024
  • Genre: Indie Folk / Acoustic

Why I Love It & Song Meaning

An acoustic indie-folk ballad driven by raw, passionate vocal delivery and evocative nautical imagery. It expresses devotion and unconditional dedication to someone through stormy tides and personal struggles.

Listen to Song

5. Earrings

Song Details

  • Title: Earrings
  • Theme: Nostalgia, Keepsakes, and Gentle Memories
  • Genre: Indie / Alternative

Why I Love It & Song Meaning

A soothing and contemplative song about small physical keepsakes that hold huge emotional value. Like a piece of jewelry left behind, small objects often become timeless tokens of people, conversations, and moments we hold dear.
