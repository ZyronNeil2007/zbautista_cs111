📁 My Favorite Movies

Author: Zyron Neil A. Bautista | Course: CS111 - Introduction to Computing

Introduction

Cinema has an extraordinary ability to transport us into different worlds, evoke profound emotions, and tell unforgettable stories. From thrilling superhero blockbusters to heartfelt romantic dramas, here is a showcase of my top favorite movies.

1. Red, White & Royal Blue (2023)

Film Details

  • Title: Red, White & Royal Blue
  • Release Year: 2023
  • Genre: Romantic Comedy, Drama
  • Director: Matthew López
  • Based on: Novel by Casey McQuiston

Synopsis & Cast

Alex Claremont-Diaz, the charismatic son of the first female President of the United States, has a long-standing feud with Britain's Prince Henry. After a public disaster threatens US-British relations, the two are forced into a staged truce. As they spend time together, their frosty rivalry gradually blossoms into a secret, passionate romance that could complicate their public duties.

Main Cast: Taylor Zakhar Perez (Alex), Nicholas Galitzine (Prince Henry), Uma Thurman (President Ellen Claremont), Rachel Hilson (Nora).

2. Call Me by Your Name (2017)

Film Details

  • Title: Call Me by Your Name
  • Release Year: 2017
  • Genre: Romantic Drama, Coming-of-Age
  • Director: Luca Guadagnino

Synopsis & Cast

Set during the sun-drenched summer of 1983 in northern Italy, 17-year-old Elio Perlman develops a passionate, transformative relationship with Oliver, a charming 24-year-old American graduate student working as an intern for Elio's father. The film is renowned for its breathtaking cinematography, evocative soundtrack, and deeply emotional exploration of first love and heartbreak.

Main Cast: Timothée Chalamet (Elio Perlman), Armie Hammer (Oliver), Michael Stuhlbarg (Mr. Perlman), Amira Casar (Annella).

3. Marry My Dead Body (2022)

Film Details

  • Title: Marry My Dead Body (關於我和鬼變成家人的那件事)
  • Release Year: 2022
  • Genre: Action, Comedy, Supernatural, Mystery
  • Director: Cheng Wei-hao

Synopsis & Cast

Homophobic police officer Wu Ming-han accidentally picks up a red wedding envelope while gathering evidence, only to find himself bound in a ghost marriage to Mao Mao, a deceased gay man. To sever the spiritual knot, Ming-han must partner with his ghostly spouse to solve an unsolved crime, leading to hilarious misadventures, touching moments, and unexpected mutual empathy.

Main Cast: Greg Hsu (Wu Ming-han), Austin Lin (Mao Pang-yu / Mao Mao), Gingle Wang (Lin Tzu-ching).

4. Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

Film Details

  • Title: Avengers: Infinity War
  • Release Year: 2018
  • Genre: Action, Sci-Fi, Adventure
  • Directors: Anthony Russo & Joe Russo

Synopsis & Cast

The Avengers and their allies must sacrifice everything in an attempt to defeat the powerful Thanos before his blitz of devastation and ruin puts an end to half of all life in the universe. An epic spectacle of unmatched cinematic scale and dramatic stakes.

Main Cast: Robert Downey Jr. (Iron Man), Chris Hemsworth (Thor), Mark Ruffalo (Hulk), Chris Evans (Captain America), Scarlett Johansson (Black Widow), Josh Brolin (Thanos).

5. Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022)

Film Details

  • Title: Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness
  • Release Year: 2022
  • Genre: Fantasy, Action, Sci-Fi, Horror
  • Director: Sam Raimi

Synopsis & Cast

Doctor Stephen Strange teams up with a mysterious teenage girl from his dreams who can travel across alternate realities to battle multiple threats, including dangerous other-universe versions of himself and the grief-stricken Scarlet Witch.

Main Cast: Benedict Cumberbatch (Doctor Strange), Elizabeth Olsen (Wanda Maximoff / Scarlet Witch), Xochitl Gomez (America Chavez), Benedict Wong (Wong).
