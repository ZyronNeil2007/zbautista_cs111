📁 Joke Time

Author: Zyron Neil A. Bautista | Course: CS111 - Introduction to Computing

Introduction

Life can get stressful with exams, programming projects, and everyday routines. Laughter is the best way to decompress! Here is a curated selection of five clean, fun, and witty school and computer jokes to brighten your day and give you a good chuckle.

Joke #1: The Programmer's Glasses

Question: Why do programmers prefer wearing glasses?

Answer: Because they don't C#! (see sharp)

Joke #2: The Math Book's Dilemma

Question: Why was the math book always looking so sad and depressed?

Answer: Because it had way too many problems of its own to solve!

Joke #3: Cold Computer

Question: Why was the student's laptop shivering and catching a cold during the lecture?

Answer: Because somebody left too many Windows open!

Joke #4: The Lost Pencil

Question: What did one pencil say to the other after taking a long exam?

Answer: "Looking sharp! But honestly, I feel completely pointless right now."

Joke #5: The Binary Secret

Statement: There are only 10 types of people in the world...

Punchline: Those who understand binary numbers, and those who don't!

A Parting Smile for You

Remember: "A day without laughter is a day wasted." Whatever challenges or bugs you face today, keep smiling, stay positive, and take life one step at a time!
